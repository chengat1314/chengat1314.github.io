About

Demolish the maze, embrace the agentic world!

I work on Grab's data platform within Tech Infra in Singapore. The goal behind my work is simple: data made easy. Teams across the company should be able to produce, trust, find and use data without fighting the platform.

With my colleagues, I've written publicly about how Grab approaches data SLAs, real-time ingestion, LLM-powered data classification, conversational data discovery and data mesh. The latest piece covers how more than 95% of data production issues (DPIs) are now raised automatically, and automated DPIs are resolved 6x faster.

I'm increasingly focused on the economics of data infrastructure: picking the right engines and architectures so the platform stays reliable and cost-efficient as it grows.

I'm an alumnus of the National University of Singapore and I work in English and Chinese.
